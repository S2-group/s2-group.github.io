We are delighted to congratulate **Iffat Fatima** on successfully completing her PhD **cum laude**! 🎓

Iffat’s PhD research focused on the design and evaluation of **SustEval**, an expert-assisted quantitative method for evaluating software architecture from a sustainability perspective. SustEval was developed to support researchers and practitioners in systematically assessing the sustainability of architectural decisions and guiding them towards more sustainable software architectures.

We are proud of Iffat’s achievement and wish her all the best for the next chapter of her career!
